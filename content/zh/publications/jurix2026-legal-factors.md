AI & Law has long represented legal knowledge symbolically, through rules, factors, and arguments, while LLMs learn their own representations from text. We connect the two: using linear probes and sparse auto-encoders on three open models, we show that CATO factors can be read from model activations, often from a single SAE feature, and we locate each factor at a concrete layer and feature.
