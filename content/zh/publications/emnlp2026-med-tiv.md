Med-TIV trains medical reasoning verifiers to gather evidence through repeated searches of external medical corpora and produce justified judgments. It combines tool use with iterative reinforcement learning, trace-level supervision, and an adaptive training curriculum. Experiments across four medical reasoning benchmarks demonstrate improved answer accuracy and sampling efficiency when the verifier guides inference-time search.
