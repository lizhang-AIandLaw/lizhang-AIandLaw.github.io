I am a Ph.D. student in Intelligent Systems at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://www.pitt.edu/), advised by [Prof. Kevin Ashley](https://www.law.pitt.edu/people/kevin-d-ashley). In Summer 2026, I was an Applied Research Intern at Thomson Reuters Labs, working on legal agents.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as a Legal Counselor at [The Export-Import Bank of China](https://www.eximbank.gov.cn/), focusing on cross-border financing, banking transactions, and regulatory compliance. I hold a Master of  Law from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/index.htm) and a Bachelor of Electrical Engineering from [China University of Mining and Technology](https://global.cumt.edu.cn/), and studied abroad at the [Australian National University](https://www.anu.edu.au/).

My research focuses on **legal intelligence**: building LLM agents that can handle real-world, high-stakes legal work. I pursue this along two lines:

1. **Evaluation & Benchmarks.** I build benchmarks from real-world legal tasks and hold agents to the standards of legal professionals. They cover multi-turn legal consultation ([DLawBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13931)), real-world legal practice ([PLawBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16669)), precedent reasoning ([Precedent Overruling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20941)), and hierarchical rule application ([Hierarchical Legal Reasoning](https://thinking-longer-thinking-smarter.vercel.app/)). They also test whether agent outputs stay faithful to legal authority ([Faithfulness & Abstention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00694)).
2. **Training & Optimization.** At the model level, I work on post-training, including post-training for mixture-of-experts models ([DenseMixer](https://fengyao.notion.site/moe-posttraining)) and tool-integrated reinforcement learning for verification ([Tool-Integrated RL Verification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20221)). At the system level, I design multi-agent frameworks for legal argumentation ([Reflective Multi-Agent Argumentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02992)) and retrieval-augmented methods for legal annotation ([Retrieval-Based Annotation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16767)).
