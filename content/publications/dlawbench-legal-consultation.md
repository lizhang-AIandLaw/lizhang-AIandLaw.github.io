DLawBench evaluates large language models in multi-turn lawyer-client consultation, focusing on whether they elicit missing facts, identify risk points, and avoid validating ungrounded client narratives. The benchmark uses realistic client behaviors across Chinese and U.S. law to expose legal sycophancy and measure consultation-grounded legal reasoning.
